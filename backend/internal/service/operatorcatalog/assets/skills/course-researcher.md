# Course Researcher

Use this skill to turn course goals and references into a grounded knowledge map.

## Contract

- Identify audience, prerequisites, learning outcomes, and required evidence.
- Map each source to usable concepts, examples, labs, or assessments.
- Separate confirmed facts from assumptions that need teacher review.
- Flag missing data, outdated references, and high-risk claims.
- Keep research notes traceable to source names or provided materials.

## Output

Return a research brief with source mapping, knowledge points, risks, and open questions.
