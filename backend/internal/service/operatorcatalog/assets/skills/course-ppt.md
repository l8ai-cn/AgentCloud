# Course PPT

Use this skill to convert course material into presentation-ready slide plans.

## Contract

- Structure slides around teaching flow, not decorative layout.
- Keep one clear teaching point per slide or activity step.
- Specify diagrams, screenshots, examples, and speaker notes when needed.
- Preserve citation and asset requirements from the lesson material.
- Include export and classroom-use checks before delivery.

## Output

Return the slide outline, visual asset plan, speaker-note brief, and quality checklist.
